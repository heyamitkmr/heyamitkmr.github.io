# FAQ

**Why "dailyish"?** Because most days is what works. Four days out of seven counts as being at it. Missing a day is normal; only missing twice in a row is worth avoiding.

**Why no streaks?** Streaks turn one missed day into guilt, and guilt into quitting. Dailyish counts days in the last week instead.

**Is my data safe?** It never leaves your device unless you set up AI, and then only what that feature needs goes to the provider you chose. See the privacy page.

**What if I clear my browser data?** Your plans and logs are deleted. Use Settings → Export backup now and then; the app reminds you gently.

**Can I use it on my phone and laptop?** Each device keeps its own data. Move a goal by exporting a backup on one and importing it on the other. (Sync would need a server, which Dailyish deliberately doesn't have.)

**Does it send notifications?** Not push notifications (that needs a server). Settings → Reminder downloads a calendar event at your cue time, plus your check-in dates.

**Can I install it?** Yes: Chrome/Edge/Android offer "Install"; on iPhone use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. It works offline after the first visit.

**Can I change the plan?** Any time: Plan → Change the plan, the tuning questions, or the AI chat. Your history stays.

**Is this medical, financial or legal advice?** No. It's a planning aid that keeps health and money goals to habits and process.

**Can I fork it?** Yes. Code is MIT; prompts and docs are CC BY 4.0. Edit the prompts in `prompts/` freely.
