# How Dailyish works

## 1. One step to a plan
Type what you want to stick to. You get today's card straight away:
- **Without AI**, the goal is matched to a built-in template (fitness, language, instrument, writing, career, study, money, calm, building something, reading, sleep, creative work, eating, or a general habit or project) and filled in with your words.
- **With AI set up**, the same card appears instantly and is replaced by a tailored plan about 15 seconds later (if you haven't started using it yet; otherwise you're offered it).

## 2. Each day
The card shows your goal, your cue ("After I …, I …"), and one row per lane: a circle to tick and today's first step (tap to edit). Below: the minimum day ("Bad day? … That counts."), a dot for each day you showed up this week, and whether you're on track for a weekly win.

**Dailyish** means showing up on at least 4 of the last 7 days. Missed days are normal; there are no streaks to break.

## 3. Weeks, wins and check-ins
- A **weekly win** is hitting a set number of your targets. Outcomes you don't control (replies, interviews) are shown but never count against you.
- **Check-ins** (e.g. week 4, month 3) compare your totals with "keep going if" numbers and ask an honest question: is this helping?
- A **monthly re-plan** is a normal step, never a sign of failure. Your history always stays.

## 4. Make it fit you
*Make it fit you better* asks one quick question at a time (when, how much time, what makes you stop, why, your minimum, what helped before). Each answer changes the plan at once, shows what changed, and can be undone. With AI, the plan's own questions take free-text answers.

## 5. Go deeper (optional)
- **Plan it in depth with AI:** seven short stages (goal, what sticks, paths, lanes, ritual, check-ins, first step) as a chat. Each stage ends with proposed changes you accept or keep talking about.
- **Without AI in the app:** paste `prompts/00-all-in-one.md` into any chat AI, then import the plan file it gives you.

## 6. More than one goal
One is best for most people. You can run up to 4; each has its own plan, card and history. A switcher appears next to the name.

## The plan file
Everything about a goal is one JSON file (`schema/plan.schema.json`). Export it, edit it, import it, or make one with any AI using the prompt pack.
