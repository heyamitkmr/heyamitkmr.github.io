# Wayfinder

**Think it through. Find the next move.**

Wayfinder is a private guide for the thing you've been turning over for weeks. It takes you from "something's not right" to a clear view of what matters and what to try next, in your own words and at your own pace.

**[Open Wayfinder](https://YOUR-WAYFINDER-ADDRESS)**

No account. No AI. No tracking. It works offline, and what you write stays on your device.

## Get started

1. Open Wayfinder and choose **Start something new**.
2. Write what's on your mind. It doesn't need to be a clear problem yet.
3. Answer one question at a time, about ten in all. Skip anything that doesn't help.

Everything saves as you type. Want to see the end result first? Choose **See a worked example** on the home screen.

<details>
<summary><strong>Install it like an app</strong> (optional)</summary>

Installing adds Wayfinder to your home screen or app list, and helps protect your work from being cleared by the browser.

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| iPhone or iPad (Safari) | Share → **Add to Home Screen** |
| Android (Chrome) | Menu → **Add to Home screen** or **Install app** |
| Computer (Chrome or Edge) | Install icon in the address bar |

Open the site once while online. After that it works without a connection.

</details>

## How it works

Each problem you bring is a **thread**. A thread moves through five stages:

| Stage | The question | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Diagnose** | Why does it feel bad? | What's actually wrong |
| **2. Clarify** | What do you want to achieve? | What you want, separate from how you first thought of getting it |
| **3. Explore** | What are the alternatives? | A wider range of options |
| **4. Find leverage** | What would make the biggest difference? | The one thing worth your effort |
| **5. Act** | What is the next meaningful move? | A small next step, or a clear decision to wait |

You can jump to any stage at any time. "Not now", "let it go" and "still undecided" all count as real outcomes.

Want more depth? Each stage ends with a short recap and an optional **Go deeper**, with further questions if they'd help.

Stuck on a question? **Help me with this question** offers a few prompts and two or three exercises.

## Coming back later

Real problems take more than one go, so Wayfinder expects you to leave and return.

- **Take a break** whenever you like. Leave a note for your future self and, if you want, a date to come back.
- **Pick up where you left off.** Home groups your threads into Waiting for you, In progress and Parked. Inside, you'll see how long it's been and your note.
- **Check in on actions.** Go through them one at a time and say how each went. Any you leave stay waiting for next time.
- **Change your mind.** Choose **Something has changed** to record what's different. If you rewrite the problem, later stages ask whether they still hold. Earlier wording is kept in your working record.

<details>
<summary><strong>Can it remind me?</strong></summary>

Wayfinder can't send notifications, because that would need a server. When you set a return date you can add it to your own calendar as a file, and the entry doesn't mention what the thread is about. The date also shows on the home screen.

</details>

## Exercises

Wayfinder may suggest a short exercise when you're stuck. They're always optional, and suggestions come only from the options you pick. It never reads what you've written to decide.

| If you... | Try | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Feel something's wrong but can't say why | Find words for the feeling (Focusing) | 10 min |
| Keep avoiding something | Understand what you're avoiding (Aversion Factoring) | 10 min |
| Worry about how something will turn out | Explore what you expect to happen (Inner Simulator) | 10 min |
| Are in two minds | Hear out both sides of yourself (Internal Double Crux) | 15 min |
| Keep clashing with someone | Find what a disagreement is really about (Double Crux) | 15 min |
| Are attached to one solution | Unpack what you want from this (Goal Factoring) | 15 min |
| Can't tell what matters most | Find what most deserves attention (Hamming Questions) | 15 min |
| Treat one explanation as certain | Consider other explanations (Multiple Hypothesis Testing) | 15 min |
| Are going round in circles | Break the loop with five minutes of action (Resolve Cycles) | 5–15 min |
| Keep hitting the same problem | Make it easier by design (Systemization) | 15 min |
| Are stuck between good options | Choose, or find out what would settle it (Decide or Test) | 15 min |
| Fear something that's probably safe to try | Test a fear with a small experiment (Comfort Zone Expansion) | 15 min, plus the experiment |
| Have plans that tend to fall apart | Find out how this plan would fail (Murphyjitsu) | 15 min |
| Forget when the moment comes | Link the action to a moment (Trigger-Action Plans) | 5 min |

You can browse them all under **Exercises**. Each is a short side trip: your place is saved, and what you find is added to your brief when you finish. Some offer **Go further** for those who want the fuller version. Exercises that involve doing something in real life become an action, and the follow-up questions arrive when you check in on it.

## Your Thinking Brief

Your thread builds into a **Thinking Brief**: the problem, what you want, what you considered, your decision and your next actions. It's made entirely from your own words, and you can edit it directly.

Copy it, print it, or save it as a PDF, Markdown or plain text. The **Full Working Record** adds your exercises and earlier versions of your answers.

## Your privacy

- **What you write stays in this browser, on this device.** It's never uploaded, and Wayfinder has no server to send it to.
- **No account, analytics or AI.** Nothing you write is read or analysed anywhere else.
- **The site's host sees a visit,** as with any website, but never what you write.
- **Filenames and tab titles stay neutral,** so your problem doesn't show up in your downloads or browser history.

<details>
<summary><strong>What private doesn't cover</strong></summary>

Your work isn't encrypted. Anyone who can use this browser on this device could open it, and backup files are plain text. Keep backups somewhere you trust, and take care on shared computers.

</details>

## Keep a backup

Your browser holds the only copy of your work, and browsers can clear stored data. A backup file is your safety net.

Go to **Backup & data** and choose **Export all threads**. Wayfinder will remind you if you've gone a while without one.

<details>
<summary><strong>Moving between devices</strong></summary>

There's no automatic sync. Export a backup on one device, then choose **Import a backup** on the other. Wayfinder shows you what will happen first. If a thread changed on both devices, it keeps both copies rather than overwriting either.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Private windows and clearing browser data</strong></summary>

Private windows usually can't keep your work once they close, and Wayfinder warns you if that's the case. Clearing your browser's site data also deletes your threads, so export a backup first.

</details>

## What Wayfinder isn't

Wayfinder is a thinking aid. It isn't therapy, a diagnosis or advice, and it won't tell you what to value or what to do.

**If you might hurt yourself or you're in danger,** please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line now. [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) lists free crisis lines by country, and **Need more support?** in the app has more.

## Questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it cost anything?</strong></summary>

No. There's no account or sign-up.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Do I have to finish?</strong></summary>

No. Nothing expires, and you can skip any step. Threads can be parked or marked as resolved whenever you like.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I delete something?</strong></summary>

You can delete a thread and undo it for a few seconds afterwards. **Backup & data → Delete everything** removes all your threads from this browser, permanently.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I use dark mode?</strong></summary>

Yes. Wayfinder follows your device, or you can switch with the sun and moon button at the top of the screen.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Are there keyboard shortcuts?</strong></summary>

Ctrl+Enter (or Cmd+Enter on a Mac) moves you to the next step.

</details>
